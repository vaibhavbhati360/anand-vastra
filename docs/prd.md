# PRD: Anand Vastra Bhandar Website

## 1. Summary

A one-page showcase website for **Anand Vastra Bhandar**, a clothing and fabric shop.
It shows the shop's products and gets people to **call, WhatsApp, or visit the showroom**.
There is **no online buying**.

## 4. Goals

- A visitor understands within 5 seconds what the shop sells and that it is premium.
- A visitor can call or WhatsApp in one tap from any screen on a phone.
- A visitor can find the showroom address and timings without scrolling hunts.
- The owner can add or remove a product by editing one data file and dropping in a photo.

## 5. Success looks like

- More calls, WhatsApp messages and showroom visits that mention the website.
- The page loads fast on a mid-range Android phone on 4G (Lighthouse Performance ≥ 90 on mobile).

## 6. Non-goals (not in this version)

- Cart, checkout, payments, prices with "Buy" buttons
- User accounts or login
- Admin panel or database
- Blog
- Hindi version (English only for v1; Hindi may come later)

## 7. Anti-goals (failure even if it "works")

- Using **another brand's photos** (for example the model photos from the wedding-collection story, or images showing a supplier's tag like "Mitva Loyal") as if they were the shop's own.
- Using Instagram story screenshots with "Add comment…" bars or text overlays as product photos.
- A slow, heavy page that phones struggle to load.
- Sherwanis placed above suits and kurtas.
- A cheap or cluttered look. The brand is premium.

## 8. Users

- **Local customers** looking for suits, kurtas or fabric, mostly on phones.
- **Wedding shoppers** looking for kotis, sherwanis and festive fabric.
- People who found the shop on **Instagram** and want contact details or directions.
